### Post 1 (id 3481263) — Kaggle Team — 2026-06-25 19:31 — votes 13  [OFFICIAL]
Format review:
- 2 active submissions at a time; best performing = leaderboard score.
- Submit as often as you like, up to 5/day; the two MOST RECENT submissions are the active ones.
- Submissions play games continuously through competition + ~2 weeks BEYOND deadline.
- Post-deadline games serve two purposes: (a) calibrate new submissions to appropriate ranking; (b) play enough games to decrease σ so leaderboard positions converge.
- Every game meaningfully updates ranking — unfavorable/favorable early matchmaking is NEVER "locked in."
- The two submissions active at END of submission timeline are the ones used in the final calibration/convergence period.
- Kaggle does NOT intend to reset leaderboard at deadline for a final tournament, but reserves the right to do so in conjunction with the Sponsor team; would announce with plenty of notice.
- Target ~24 games/day per submission; prioritization to newer submissions and those that haven't run in a while. Matchmaking lag normal, resolves within the day.
